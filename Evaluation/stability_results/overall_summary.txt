To assess the stability of the extraction step under LLM non-determinism, we executed the extraction 3 times independently on all 17 case studies using gpt-5.2 (without HCI enhancement or human-in-the-loop feedback). Across 51 run instances, the pipeline achieved mean Structural F1 = 0.301 $\pm$ 0.160, Type F1 = 0.655 $\pm$ 0.239, and Recall = 0.892 $\pm$ 0.260, confirming that the reported results are stable and are not an artefact of a single favourable draw.
